Quick reference

Dental emergency guide

01

Protect breathing

Call 911 for breathing or swallowing difficulty, major facial trauma, or uncontrolled bleeding.

02

Protect the tooth

Handle a knocked-out permanent tooth by the crown. Keep it moist and get dental care immediately.

03

Call a dentist

For severe pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or a lost restoration, ask a dentist how quickly you should be seen.

Medical disclaimer

The Florida Dental Answer Center provides general educational information only. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend an individualized treatment, establish a dentist–patient relationship, or replace an examination by a licensed professional.
